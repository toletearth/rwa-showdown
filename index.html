📖 GAME RULES
How it works: The 52-card deck is split evenly. Each round the active caller picks a category from their top card. Higher wins — except Rank, where lower wins. Winner takes both cards to the bottom of their deck. A tie draws the next card and replays the same category, with everything on the line. Run out of cards, and it's over.
Categories: Rank (lower is better), Height, Weight, Matches, Biceps, Chest — all others, higher wins.
